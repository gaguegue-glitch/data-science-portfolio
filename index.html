Project 1: FoodAPS Household Food Spending Analysis

Research Question

How does regularly preparing and eating meals at home affect a family's physical health and monthly household budget?

Project Overview

This project uses data from the USDA FoodAPS National Household Food Acquisition and Purchase Survey to examine household food behaviors and spending.

The analysis includes data preparation, cleaning, exploratory data analysis, visualization, correlation analysis, and linear regression.

Project 2: Telecom Customer Churn Prediction

Prediction Question

Can customer characteristics and service usage patterns be used to predict whether a telecommunications customer will churn?

Project Overview

This project develops and evaluates machine-learning models for predicting customer churn in the telecommunications industry.

The dataset contains 7,043 telecommunications customers and includes customer characteristics, account information, service information, charges, and service-usage measures.

A baseline classifier, Logistic Regression, and Random Forest were evaluated using accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score. Logistic Regression was selected as the final model.
