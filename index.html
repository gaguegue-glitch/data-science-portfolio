Project 1: FoodAPS Household Food Spending Analysis

Research Question

How is the frequency of preparing dinner at home associated with household food spending among U.S. households?

Project Overview

This project uses data from the USDA FoodAPS National Household Food Acquisition and Purchase Survey to examine the relationship between preparing dinner at home and household food spending.

The analysis includes data preparation, data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, visualization, correlation analysis, and linear regression.

Project 2: Telecom Customer Churn Prediction

Prediction Question

Can customer characteristics and service usage patterns be used to predict whether a telecommunications customer will churn?

Project Overview

This project develops and evaluates machine-learning models for predicting customer churn in the telecommunications industry.

The dataset contains 7,043 telecommunications customers and includes customer characteristics, account information, service information, charges, and service-usage measures.

A baseline classifier, Logistic Regression, and Random Forest were evaluated using accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score. Confusion matrices, Random Forest feature importance, and Logistic Regression coefficients were also examined to interpret the models.

Logistic Regression was selected as the final model. It achieved an accuracy of 80.06%, precision of 67.82%, recall of 47.33%, and an F1-score of 55.75%.
