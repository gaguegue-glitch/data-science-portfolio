Projects

FoodAPS Household Food Spending Analysis

This project examines the relationship between the frequency of preparing dinner at home and household food spending among U.S. households.

Research Question

How is the frequency of preparing dinner at home associated with household food spending among U.S. households?

Project Overview

The project uses data from the U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) National Household Food Acquisition and Purchase Survey (FoodAPS). Python was used for data acquisition, cleaning, preparation, visualization, correlation analysis, and linear regression.

The complete analysis includes the problem definition, data description, data cleaning and preparation, visualizations and insights, storytelling and narrative, limitations, ethics and reflection, and code transparency.
